Use it on your own site

Easiest option is an iframe:

<iframe
  src="https://fyosamu.github.io/tools/website-cost-calculator/"
  style="width:100%;min-height:820px;border:0"
  loading="lazy"
  title="Website cost calculator"></iframe>

Or copy the <style>, markup and bottom <script> straight into your page — the whole calculation is one IIFE using plain selectors like #type and #price.

Change the prices

  • Website calculator — base and baseWeeks objects in the script set the starting price and delivery per project type. Feature prices are attributes on the checkboxes themselves: data-price="180", data-weeks="1".
  • Bot calculator — base / baseW objects for the six bot types; volume surcharges live on the <option> values.
  • Market range — a handful of ratio multipliers on the total. Change the ratios, not the output variables.

Why they were built

Most agency sites hide pricing behind a contact form, because the usual playbook is quote high, negotiate down, hope nobody compares you. This studio publishes every price and built the calculator so you can get a number without talking to anyone. The market ranges are indicative — they exist so you can check a quote, not as a promise from anyone.
