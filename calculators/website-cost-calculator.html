Questions this calculator cannot answer

Why is a website ever $450?

Because it is one person with no account managers, no sales commission and no office overhead, using a proven build instead of rebuilding the wheel every time. What you do not pay for is the agency around the work. What you still get is the same deliverable and the same accountability.

What makes a quote jump from the low end to the high end?

Logins, payments and integrations. Anything that has to remember who you are, take money, or talk to another system roughly doubles the work behind a page, and the estimate above reflects that.

Is USDT payment actually workable for a business?

Yes, and it is the only way we can take payment reliably from several countries. We use BEP-20 or ERC-20 only — never TRC-20, because the fee behaviour and support vary too much. You receive a wallet address and a fixed amount before anything starts.

Do I need to book a call to get the real price?

No. That is the point of this page. Send the scope, get the number, decide. If the number does not fit, you have lost two minutes rather than an afternoon.
