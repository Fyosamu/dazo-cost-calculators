Common questions

How long does a Telegram bot actually take?

Most of what people ask for is three to seven days of work. The things that extend it are payments and anything that has to talk to a system you already run, because those are the parts that can fail in ways nobody notices until a customer does.

Do I need a server to run it?

Yes, but it is smaller than people imagine. A well-written bot handling tens of thousands of messages a month fits comfortably on a small VPS. The hosting line above is a realistic range rather than a promotional number.

Who owns the code?

You do. The repository and the deployment are handed over, so you are never renting your own bot from us.

Can you take over a bot someone else wrote?

Yes. Send the repository and a description of what is broken. Partial rewrites are quoted as maintenance rather than a new build.

Why is payment in USDT?

Because cards and PayPal are unreliable or unavailable for the countries many of our clients are in. We use BEP-20 or ERC-20 only — never TRC-20. The amount is fixed before work begins.
